Design mockup · Gabriel for Denver

The Field Guide Bands

Direction B from Issues at Two Speeds, drawn out at real dimensions. One promise per full-bleed band, a number or a pull-quote as the hero, and the policy detail in a sheet that is still a plain, linkable, printable document underneath.

Grounded against main @ 07970fe — 13 Sept 2026  ·  plan.html  ·  schema.html

01

The unit is a plank

A plank is one promise: one thing Gabriel will do in office. It gets one band, one screen, one anchor, and one entry in the CMS. Everything on this page is built out of that one repeated object, which is what makes both the design and the content model tractable.

Band anatomy, top to bottom. Every part maps to one CMS field — see schema.html.
PartWhat it doesType
kickerLocates the plank: guide name, then its sub-topic. The only breadcrumb a band gets.0.7rem / 0.24em caps
heroA statistic, or a pull-quote when there is no defensible number. The only thing on the band carrying visual weight, and the whole reason it is set this big.13rem desktop / 6.5rem phone
captionSays what the number counts. Never optional on a stat band — a bare figure is a rumour.1.6rem / 300 / 18ch
commitmentThe promise, under an I WILL eyebrow. Written as a verb phrase, always.2.45rem / 600
whyOne sentence of justification. Always visible — this is what keeps the sheet from hiding the argument.1.18rem / 300 / 34ch
actionOpens the sheet. Also a real link to #plank-<slug>, so it survives JS being off.0.98rem / 600
sourceFootnote scoped to this band, because the band is the shareable unit. Absent until the citation exists — see the gold band.0.76rem underlined

Three band themes, cycled. ink → sky → gold, repeating, with a per-plank override. (This note first said ink → sky → ink → gold, which contradicted the frames below and meant a three-plank guide never reached gold; changed after the pages went live.) On every ground the hero numeral is set in whichever brand colour isn't the ground or the text — yellow on the blue band, blue on the pale band, navy on the gold band. That rule is what makes the giant figure read as deliberate rather than as a highlight.

Bands carry no illustration. An earlier draft put the guide's SVG behind the numeral as an 8% watermark; that was dropped. A band of nothing but a 13rem numeral on a flat brand colour is the more confident poster, and it is closer to how this language has to work on Instagram, where these are going to be screenshotted. The six illustrations keep their full size on the guide hero and on the index cards — they are not being retired, they are being put where they can be themselves.

It also fixes a live bug by construction: src/pages/issues/[id].astro picks its band colour with a hardcoded ternary chain that runs out at index 2, so a fourth sub-issue renders with no background at all. A modulo over a themes array cannot run out.

The sheet is a <details> in the HTML. Direction B's one real weakness, in its own words, was that the detail is “off-page in a sheet, which reads as an afterthought and is harder to link and to print.” That is only true if the sheet is built as a JavaScript overlay. Build it as a server-rendered <details> instead and all three problems go away at once: with JS off it is an ordinary inline disclosure, it prints as a normal document, crawlers read it as page text, and deep-linking to #plank-<slug> opens it natively. The ~40 lines of progressive enhancement only promote that element into a fixed panel and push the anchor into history.

The gold band has no footnote, on purpose. The campaign is shipping figures uncited for now and backfilling citations as they are found, so the design needs an honest render for an unsourced number — it is the source line simply not being there, with nothing moving around it. sources is a one-field edit per plank, so a band gains its footnote the moment someone finds the study.

05

Interaction, and what happens when it fails

Every behaviour below degrades to something that still works. That is the whole reason the detail is a <details> and not a modal.

BehaviourWith JSWithout JS
open detailPromotes the open <details> to a fixed sheet, moves focus to its heading, marks the rest inert.Expands inline, in flow.
deep linkhistory.replaceState writes #plank-<slug>; arriving on that URL opens the sheet.Browser opens the ancestor <details> and scrolls to it.
closeEsc, the ×, scrim click, and Back all close it.The disclosure toggle closes it.
printA print stylesheet forces every <details> open and un-fixes the sheet, so the guide prints as a plain document.
crawl / shareAll detail prose is in the server-rendered HTML, so it is indexed and it feeds the meta description.
motionprefers-reduced-motion drops the slide transform; the sheet cross-fades instead.

Analytics

Two new events, in the repo’s existing snake_case, optional-chained, is:inline style, registered in .posthog-events.json alongside the rest: plank_detail_opened (guide_slug, plank_slug) and platform_guide_viewed. The first one is the point — it tells the campaign which promises people actually open, which is a thing the current page cannot answer at all. Both fire on the Spanish routes too, unlike issue_viewed, which today is missing from src/pages/es/issues/[id].astro entirely.

06

What this fixes on the way past

Four things in the current issue pages that this design cannot reproduce even by accident, because the shape it needs rules them out.

  • The fourth-band fall-through. Today’s band colour is an index ternary that returns undefined past index 2, so a fourth sub-issue renders on bare white. A modulo over a themes array has no fourth case to forget.
  • Hardcoded slug→image and slug→alt maps. Adding an issue today means editing two page files. Artwork and its alt text become schema fields with image(), the way posts and events already do it.
  • The English/Spanish drift. One band.astro taking a lang prop, rather than the current pair of near-identical page files that have already drifted — the Spanish issue page silently has no analytics, and its index anchor is #retos while every back-link points at #what-will-i-tackle.
  • Unlinkable detail. Nothing inside a sub-issue has an anchor today; only the sub-issue heading does. Every plank gets its own.

None of it touches the live pages. src/content/issues/, /issues and its Spanish twin are not modified by any of this — the new surface is a separate collection, a separate folder, and a separate route, and the two run side by side until the campaign decides to cut over. The fixes above land on the new pages; the old ones are retired rather than repaired.

Companion documents: plan.html (approach, PRs, open questions) and schema.html (the two new collections, field by field). Every open decision is numbered in the plan, not here.
